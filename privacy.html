Insomnia: Survive the Night

Privacy Policy · effective 22 October 2026

Insomnia is a game by 4M Studios IKE (4m-studios.com). This page explains what the game stores and sends, and why. The short version: no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. The only thing that leaves your device is the online leaderboard, which uses a random player number and a made-up nickname, never your name.

What stays on your device

Your progress (Sand, your bedroom, dreams reached, achievements, settings and your best scores) is saved only on your device. On Android it can be included in your own Google backup if you have backups switched on. In the web version it is kept in your browser's local storage.

The online leaderboard

When you are online, the game posts each finished night to a public leaderboard so you can compare scores. It sends:

DataWhat it is
Player numberA random number created by the game on your device the first time it runs. It is not linked to your Google account, phone number, advertising ID or any other identifier.
NicknameA name the game makes up from a fixed word list (for example "Restless Moth 417"). You can draw a new one at any time. You cannot type your own, so it never contains personal information.
Night resultsScore, kills, health left at dawn, the dream, whether Nightmare mode was on, and the game version.

Other players can see the nickname and the results on the leaderboard. The data is stored by our database provider, Supabase, which processes it only for us. We do not sell it, share it with anyone else, or use it for advertising. It is kept while the leaderboard runs. To have your scores removed, email us your nickname (shown in the Leaderboard screen) and we will delete them.

Purchases

"The Full Dream" is bought through Google Play, and Google processes the payment under the Google Privacy Policy. The game only checks on your device whether you own it; we never see your payment details.

Children

The game has dark, dreamlike horror themes and is not directed at children under 13.

Deleting your data

Use "Forget everything" in the game's settings, or uninstall the game, to delete everything stored on your device. For leaderboard scores, see above.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date. Questions: info@4m-studios.com.
